I build identity infrastructure that doesn't watch you.

I’m CTO at Yivi, the privacy-first identity wallet, and at Caesar Groep. Two problems occupy me: making digital identity work at national scale without building a surveillance layer, and rebuilding how engineering teams operate now that agents write most of the first draft.

Before this I spent a decade as a cloud architect, mostly deep in Azure — search scoring, serverless integration, governance frameworks. That writing is still in the archive and still gets read, which is either a compliment or a comment on the documentation.
